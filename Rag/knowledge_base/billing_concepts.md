# Healthcare Claims and Provider Concepts

## Provider

A healthcare provider is an entity or professional associated with delivering healthcare services and submitting claims for reimbursement.

---

## Beneficiary

A beneficiary is an individual receiving healthcare benefits.

---

## Claim

A claim represents a request for payment associated with healthcare services provided to a beneficiary.

---

## Inpatient Claim

An inpatient claim represents healthcare services associated with an inpatient stay.

Relevant information may include:

- Admission date
- Discharge date
- Diagnosis information
- Procedure information
- Reimbursement
- Deductible

---

## Outpatient Claim

An outpatient claim represents healthcare services provided without an associated inpatient stay.

Relevant information may include:

- Service dates
- Diagnosis information
- Procedure information
- Reimbursement
- Deductible

---

## Reimbursement

Reimbursement represents an amount associated with payment for a healthcare claim or service.

A high reimbursement value by itself does not establish fraud. It should be interpreted in context.

---

## Deductible

A deductible represents an amount associated with the beneficiary's cost-sharing responsibility under the applicable coverage arrangement.

---

## Diagnosis Code

A diagnosis code represents information describing a patient's condition or reason associated with healthcare services.

---

## Procedure Code

A procedure code identifies a healthcare procedure or service associated with a claim.

Procedure frequency and distribution can be useful when analyzing provider behavior.

---

## Claim Duration

For inpatient claims, claim duration can be derived from the admission and discharge dates.

Example:

Admission:
2025-01-01

Discharge:
2025-01-10

Approximate duration:
9 days

Duration should be interpreted in the context of the underlying healthcare services and patient condition.
